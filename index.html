IBM Research

Sub-1nm nanostack CMOS

  • Advanced sub-1nm (7Å) bonded-stacked FET integration through EUV/immersion lithography, production tapeouts, and DRC-compliant logic and backside-power macros.
  • Led lithography NPI and process optimization, combining overlay/EPE analysis, OPC and resist qualification, CD-SEM metrology, and SPC/DOE to resolve patterning challenges.
  • Enabled wafer-bonding alignment through mark redesign and CPE/i-HOPC corrections; built Python/KLayout workflows for test-macro design and CDU analysis.
IBM Research

Advanced packaging & AI hardware

  • Delivered IBM packaging targets for patterning, CDU, electrical performance, and overlay; collaborated with and trained Rapidus engineers on 2nm-generation 3D packaging.
  • Developed DUV/i-line reticle-stitched fields of 26 × 33 mm and 52 × 52 mm for RDL wiring, bonding pads, and TSVs.
  • Reduced interposer overlay residuals by 58%, resolved alignment through grainy metal pads, and developed Cu–Cu hybrid-bonding patterning.
IBM Research

GAA nanosheets & backside power

  • Co-developed MOL/BEOL patterning with Samsung for 48 nm-pitch, stepped-channel stacked FETs, showing approximately 6% iso-power performance improvement in simulation.
  • Developed overlay corrections enabling backside power distribution in advanced nanosheet transistors.
IBM Research

Collaboration & technology transfer

  • Led ASML collaborations on lithography, overlay/EPE methodologies, and FEOL/MOL/BEOL DFM and PDK development for sub-1nm nanostacks.
  • Co-designed alignment marks for 2.5D/3D integration, TSVs, and AI hardware; validated overlay and defect metrology using KLA OVALiS.
  • Transferred lithography and overlay expertise through technical presentations and training for IBM and Rapidus engineering teams.
Purdue · Birck Nanotechnology Center

Plasmonic color & disordered metasurfaces

  • Pioneered and patented a method of generating bright colors using semicontinuous metal films under the supervision of Prof. Alexandra Boltasseva, Prof. Vladimir Shalaev and Prof Alexander Kildishev in collaboration with Military Institute of Technology, Poland. The method is environment-friendly, non-fading, lithography free, and inexpensive
  • Built a laser scanning setup for thermal ablation with Ti:Sapphire femtosecond laser to fabricate disordered metasurfaces for plasmonic colors
  • Collaborated with theorists to develop a time-domain compatible approximation for the optical response of disordered metasurfaces
Purdue · Birck Nanotechnology Center

Perovskite nanolasing & ultrafast dynamics

  • Designed compact hybrid plasmonic–photonic nanolasers using perovskite gain media in collaboration with Prof. Letian Dou.
  • Modeled metasurfaces in 2D and 3D using COMSOL wave and ray optics.
  • Investigated ultrafast carrier dynamics in thin metals and 2D/quasi-2D materials through pump–probe spectroscopy with Richard D. Schaller at Argonne National Laboratory.
Purdue · Birck Nanotechnology Center

Epsilon-near-zero & space-time materials

  • Developed novel space-time metamaterials with highly tunable photonic materials in collaboration with Prof. Mordechai Segev, Technion
  • Fabricated tunable metasurfaces with zinc oxide for dynamic polarization modulation with Prof. Doug Werner, Penn State CEARL
Purdue · Birck Nanotechnology Center

Quantum emitters & plasmonic nanocavities

  • Fabricated quantum emitters coupled to plasmonic nanostructures for single-photon sources in collaboration with Prof. Simeon I. Bogdanov, UIUC.
